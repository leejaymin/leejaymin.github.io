- Period: Aug. 2026 – Jan. 2028.

미숙아 질환 진단 AI 모델 개발

The goal of this project is to develop AI models that assist in diagnosing diseases in premature infants. Premature infants in the NICU are monitored through a heterogeneous mix of continuous vital signs, medical images, laboratory results, and EMR records, and the clinically important conditions — respiratory disorders, neurologic injury, infection, and growth complications — often show only subtle early signals across these modalities.

Working with clinicians from the Biomedical Research Institute, this project builds multimodal diagnostic models that combine these signals for early detection and risk prediction, with an emphasis on explainable inference and on models efficient enough to be deployed at the bedside. The resulting models are evaluated against clinical data to validate their usefulness as decision support.
